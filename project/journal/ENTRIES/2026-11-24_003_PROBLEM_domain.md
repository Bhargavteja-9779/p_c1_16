# 2026-11-24 — PROBLEM: the domain I am entering

Critical Care publishes prediction-model work on ICU data. The practical problem with such models, widely discussed in the field, is that a model built in one hospital often degrades in another (different populations, measurement practices, missingness patterns). I have not yet verified specific claims from the literature; that is the next step (Phase 2). Open data with more than one hospital exists on PhysioNet (e.g. the 2019 sepsis challenge, two hospital systems), which would let me test cross-site behaviour honestly on CPU.
