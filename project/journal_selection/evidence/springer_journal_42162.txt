Overview

Energy Informatics is an interdisciplinary open-access journal focused on the application of digital technology and information management to facilitate sustainable and resilient energy systems.

Provides a unique focus on the application of digital technology to energy systems.
Affiliated with two high profile conference series, ensuring maximum visibility and reach.
Caters to a broad audience, including industry, for higher citations and visibility.
Publishes original, peer-reviewed research from various scientific, technological, engineering, and social fields.
Promotes the global transition towards sustainable and resilient energy systems.
