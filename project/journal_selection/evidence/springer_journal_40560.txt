Overview
Journal of Intensive Care is an open-access journal covering all aspects of intensive care medicine, including trauma, pediatric care, emergency medicine, and organ dysfunction. It also welcomes submissions on the cultural aspects of intensive care practice.
 
Focuses on all aspects of intensive care medicine and practice including cultural aspects.
Official journal of the Japanese Society of Intensive Care Medicine.
Open access: freely and permanently available for everyone to read and reuse.
Indexed in PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, DOAJ and more.
