Overview

First launched in 2008, the Journal of Hematology & Oncology now ranks in the top quartile of journals worldwide in both of these closely intertwined and rapidly evolving fields. Encompassing all aspects of hematology and oncology, this peer reviewed, open access journal publishes high quality articles from leading experts. As the official journal of the Chinese American Hematologist and Oncologist Network (CAHON), we pride ourselves on providing top-class peer review with rapid turnaround times.
