## Why this journal
1. It is one of only four journals out of the 65 we measured whose verified median submission→acceptance is ≤ 72 days, and it still passes (66 d) after removing letters.
2. Among those four it has the best fit for a CPU-tractable, open-data machine-learning study: open ICU datasets (PhysioNet) exist with multiple hospitals, enabling a real cross-site generalization test. Cardiovascular Diabetology (JIF 15.6, median 62 d) has a higher IF, but an ML study there would need open cardio-metabolic cohort data with outcomes, which we judged a weaker fit; Energy Informatics (65 d) shows no JIF; Journal of Intensive Care (69 d, JIF 7.5) is the fallback.
3. Rule status: G1 **passes on the ≤72-day rule (verified)** and **reputable publisher (verified)**; **JCR Q1 is UNVERIFIED** because JCR is paywalled — this caveat is carried in every gate table.
